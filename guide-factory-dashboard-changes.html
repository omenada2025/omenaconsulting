Context

1. Why are we changing anything?

Leadership formalized how product ownership works in the AI Product Factory:

  • Live PM (Builder) owns delivery through the six-week cycle and handover.
  • After release and accepted handover, the Live PM moves to the next project.
  • Support PM accepts the product and owns portfolio, support, training/demos, and sustaining work.

Our dashboard already supports weekly status, portfolio health, and governance. It does not yet show Live vs Support ownership, the six-week factory runway, or handover kits. We will extend this app — we are not building a second system.

Decision already recommended: improve the current dashboard. Do not create a new application.

Factory

5. Six-week delivery cycle (reminder)

Clock starts at formal V0 handover to the Live PM (engineering supplies V0 before this). Gates use evidence at the end of Weeks 1, 3, 5, and 6.

WeekFocusKey evidence / output
1Discover and baselineObserved features and workflows · Gate
2Assess and define MSPFindings and Minimum Sellable Product scope
3Prioritize and define TO-BEBacklog and acceptance criteria · Gate
4Coordinate implementationApproved changes and resolved questions
5Validate and prepare enablementQA evidence · Support PM rehearsal · Gate
6Release and handoverSupport PM acceptance · ownership transfer · Gate

Essential blockers require a no-go and escalation. Optional scope can move to a future backlog.

Release

6. Handover — when ownership moves

Ownership does not move on release day alone. The Support PM must accept handover. Until then, Live PM closes gaps.

Three kits (from the handbook) will be tracked in the dashboard:

  • Launch Kit — use cases, approved demo, FAQ, limitations
  • Run Kit — operational instructions, rollback, monitoring, escalation
  • Live Product Kit — guides, release notes, known issues, backlog

Also required: QA/regression evidence, reverse demo by Support PM, working access, recorded accepting Support PM + effective date.

FAQ

9. Common questions

Are we replacing the dashboard?

No. Same application, extended to match the factory operating model.

Do I stop writing weekly updates?

No. Weekly reporting remains the operating rhythm. Factory cycle is an extra layer for products in the six-week path.

What happens to the Training role we added?

Training/enablement work continues, but ownership after release follows the handbook: it sits with the Support PM, not as a separate peer product-owner role.

Will my old reports break?

No. Historical reports stay. We may show clearer labels (e.g. Live vs Support) going forward and map legacy values carefully.

Where can I see the future UI?

Open How it looks after implementation. For decision detail, use the approval pack.

Who do I ask if something is unclear?

Process / ownership: Daniela (or named Ops Manager). Tool questions: same channel you use for dashboard support today.
