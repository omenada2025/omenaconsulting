Product language

Use these terms consistently in screens, meetings, exports, and support conversations. English is the product's primary operational language.

ProductThe portfolio-level offering. Avoid “project” when referring to the product itself.
WorkstreamA track of delivery within a product, owned and reported independently.
Weekly updateThe status record submitted for one product workstream and reporting week.
Functional roleProduct ownership: Live PM (Builder), Support PM, or UI/UX contributor (AI Product Factory model).
Delivery healthOn Track, In Progress, At Risk, or Paused; always pair color with text.
Governance gateA readiness decision supported by evidence, approver, rationale, and conditions.
