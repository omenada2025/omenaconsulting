3. Keep design evidence current

3

In Product Governance → evidence checklist for UI/UX, keep artifacts such as:

  • Research summary / user insights
  • Wireframes / high-fidelity designs
  • Prototype / usability validation
  • Design review sign-off · Developer handoff package

These feed Live PM gates (especially design handoff into build / validate).
